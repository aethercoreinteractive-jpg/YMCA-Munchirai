More Than a Business

MyFood is a social enterprise of Munchirai YMCA, created to generate sustainable income while contributing towards meaningful charitable and community initiatives.

Through this venture, we aim to build a responsible business model where commercial activity serves a greater purpose. A portion of the proceeds from MyFood contributes towards the YMCA’s charitable activities, social development programmes, and initiatives that create positive impact in the wider community.

Our work brings together business and social responsibility — creating value for our customers while helping support the purpose and ongoing work of Munchirai YMCA.

✨ "Every purchase contributes to something beyond the product itself."
