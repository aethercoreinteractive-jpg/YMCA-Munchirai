About My Food Products

For a Greater Purpose

My Food Products is a social enterprise of Munchirai YMCA, established to provide quality food products while generating sustainable support for community welfare initiatives. A portion of the proceeds from the business contributes towards the YMCA’s charitable activities and social development programmes.

Every purchase helps create a meaningful social impact.
