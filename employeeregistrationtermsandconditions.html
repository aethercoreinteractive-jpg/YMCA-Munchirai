Staff Terms of Service

Rules, duties, code of conduct, and employment expectations.

1.1 Application & Registration Accuracy

By submitting your credentials through the MY Food staff registration portal, you certify under penalty of internal review that all information provided—including your legal name, contact credentials, identification proofs, address history, and professional references—is completely accurate, truthful, and up-to-date. Falsification or intentional omission of credentials will result in immediate disqualification or instant termination of employment if found post-registration.

1.2 Background Verification & Compliance

You grant authorization to MY Food administration and YMCA Munchirai management to conduct standard background checks, verification of identity, and authenticity evaluations of submitted documents required for community food safety standards and institutional compliance.

1.3 Professional Conduct & Food Safety Protocol

As an active staff member or community volunteer representing MY Food, you are required to hold yourself to elite standards of conduct:

  • Strict Hygiene Standards: Strict adherence to food handling, cleanliness, and kitchen safety regulations at all times.
  • Respectful Interaction: Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior toward colleagues, managers, or community members.
  • Punctuality & Shift Management: Fulfilling assigned community distribution duties, inventory management tasks, or kitchen shifts responsibly.

1.4 Intellectual Property & Proprietary Recipes

Any operational recipes, distribution lists, software tools, digital dashboards, or operational logistics designed during your association with MY Food remain the exclusive intellectual property of YMCA Munchirai. Unauthorized duplication, commercial exploitation, or external leakages are heavily penalized.

1.5 Termination of Staff Privileges

MY Food reserves the unilateral right to suspend, terminate, or revoke staff registration access at any time for policy breaches, gross negligence, security violations, or failure to perform assigned civic and organizational responsibilities.

Staff Privacy Policy

How we collect, secure, utilize, and protect your personal data.

2.1 Commitment to Data Protection

MY Food and YMCA Munchirai deeply respect your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, process, and safeguard your personal data submitted via our digital registration panel.

2.2 Information We Collect

To safely onboard staff members, we collect specific categories of personal information:

Personal Identifiers

Full name, date of birth, residential address, telephone number, and active email address.

Administrative & Official Records

Identity document references, emergency contact lists, profile photos, and operational credentials.

2.3 Purpose of Data Usage

Your data is processed strictly for internal administrative governance, security tracking, background validation, organizational communication, scheduling, and emergency management. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal profile data to third-party marketing networks.

2.4 Data Security Infrastructure

We deploy database encryption protocols, restricted access controls via secure cloud platforms and internal credential barriers to ensure your personal records are completely shielded from unauthorized access, modification, or data leaks.

2.5 Your Rights & Inquiries

You retain the right to review, update, or correct your personal data records by submitting a direct written request to our administrative office. For compliance clarifications, contact management at YMCA Munchirai.
