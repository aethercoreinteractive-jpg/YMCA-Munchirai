Staff Code of Conduct & Food Safety

As a representative of MY Food, you are required to maintain the highest standards of hygiene, professionalism, and integrity.

  • Hygiene & Safety: Strict adherence to local health regulations, food handling safety guidelines, and personal sanitation protocols is mandatory.
  • Workplace Etiquette: Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or unsafe behavior in any MY Food facility or digital workspace.
  • Punctuality & Duty: Adhering strictly to assigned schedules, shifts, and operating policies outlined by management.

Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

During your association with MY Food, you may gain access to proprietary information, trade secrets, recipes, operational processes, customer databases, and financial data.

You agree to keep all such information strictly confidential, both during your employment and indefinitely after your departure. Unauthorized sharing, copying, or commercial exploitation of MY Food property will lead to stringent legal action.
