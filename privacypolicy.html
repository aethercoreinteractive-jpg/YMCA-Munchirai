Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our charity food distribution platform, we may collect personal details such as your full name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and landmark instructions to successfully process and fulfill your requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for processing orders, coordinating local delivery, communicating order updates, and maintaining a secure community administration record. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security & Storage

We utilize secure database infrastructure to store transaction data safely. Access to personal and order records is strictly restricted to authorized platform administrators.

4. External Media & Links

Our platform may display imagery or files hosted via external services like Google Drive. We are not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of external third-party websites or storage networks linked within our announcements.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our charitable initiatives or technological framework. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact the administration team directly through YMCA Munchirai channels.
